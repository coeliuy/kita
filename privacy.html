Privacy

Kita is in early preview. This page explains, in plain English, what Kita stores about you, where it’s kept, who can see it and how to delete it. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Who runs Kita

Kita is run by Kita App, based in the UK. If you have a question about your data, email coeli@dahildigital.com. We’re the “controller” of your account data under UK data protection law.

What Kita stores

  • Your account: your email address and password. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it.
  • What you enter: your clients and their billing details, time entries, tasks, work categories, invoices, expenses, your business details for invoices, goals and tax settings.
  • Feedback you send through the “Send feedback” box, with your email address so we can reply.
  • In your browser: your sign-in session and your light or dark mode choice. Kita doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and doesn’t use analytics.

If you add details about your clients (names, email addresses, postal addresses), you’re responsible for having a good reason to hold them, as you would in any invoicing tool.

Why

We use your data only to run Kita for you: to sign you in, save your work, and work out your figures. The legal basis is providing the service you signed up for. We use feedback to improve Kita. We don’t sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

Where it’s kept, and who can see it

  • Your data is stored by Supabase, in their London data centre.
  • The website is delivered by Cloudflare.
  • Sign-up and password emails are sent by an email delivery service on our behalf.

Each account can only see its own data: the database itself blocks anyone else’s requests. The people running Kita can technically reach the database to keep it working, and will only look at your data to fix a problem or if you ask us to.

How long it’s kept

Until you delete it. During the preview, Kita doesn’t keep backups, so please use Settings → Download everything now and then to keep your own copy.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Your account → Delete my account. Your account and everything in it are deleted straight away and can’t be recovered. Feedback you sent is kept without your email address.

Your rights

You can download everything Kita holds about you (Settings → Download everything), correct it in the app, or delete it. You can also ask us about your data at coeli@dahildigital.com. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Tax figures

Kita’s tax and take-home figures are estimates, not tax advice. Check your actual bill with HMRC or an accountant.
