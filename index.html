Kita

Kita is Filipino for earnings.

It also means “to see”. That’s the idea behind the app: see where your time goes, and see what it’s really worth once expenses and tax come out.

Most time trackers stop at the timesheet.

The harder part of freelancing comes after. A retainer quietly runs over. An invoice slips past its due date. January arrives with a tax bill bigger than you planned for. Kita connects your hours to the money side, so none of that takes you by surprise.

Track time without thinking about it

A timer that keeps running if you close the tab, plus quick entries for time you forgot to log. Type 1:30, 1.5 or 90m.

Stay on top of retainers

See hours used against hours agreed for every retainer. Kita warns you at 80% and tells you when to bill for extra hours.

Know your real hourly rate

Fixed fees and retainers hide what a client really pays you per hour. Kita shows it for every client, every month.

Invoice from your tracked time

Turn a month of work into an invoice in seconds, download it as a PDF, and see what’s owed, overdue and due next.

Log expenses as you go

Record business costs in HMRC’s categories, so your totals line up with your tax return.

See what you actually take home

A running estimate of Income Tax and National Insurance, how much to set aside, and how you’re pacing against your earnings goal.

How to use Kita

Ten minutes to set up, then a few seconds a day.

  1. Add your clients

    Choose how you bill each one: hourly, a monthly retainer with included hours, or a fixed project fee.

  2. Track your time

    Start the timer when you begin a task, or add time afterwards. Mark anything you can’t bill as not billable.

  3. Check in each week

    Overview shows your hours by day and by client, how much of each retainer is used, and what each client really pays per hour.

  4. Invoice at the end of the month

    Pick a client and a month, and Kita fills in the lines from your tracked time. Edit anything, create the invoice and download the PDF. Mark it paid when the money arrives.

  5. Log expenses when they happen

    Subscriptions, equipment, travel. It takes seconds and saves a scramble before your tax return.

  6. Check your money

    Add any salary, set a goal for the year, and move the suggested amount into your tax savings each month.

Questions

Where is my data kept?

Your clients, time and invoices are private to you. You can download everything as a backup from Settings at any time.

How accurate is the tax estimate?

It’s an estimate, not advice. It uses the 2026/27 rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, includes Class 4 National Insurance, and accounts for a salary if you add one. Like HMRC’s default cash basis, it counts income when you’re paid. It doesn’t yet cover Scottish tax bands, student loans or pension relief.

Does it work with Making Tax Digital?

Kita groups your income and expenses into the Making Tax Digital quarters and HMRC’s expense categories, and exports them as a CSV. It doesn’t send updates to HMRC directly.

Can I use my data in Google Sheets?

Yes. Download your time entries or quarterly totals as a CSV from Settings or Money, then import the file into Sheets.
