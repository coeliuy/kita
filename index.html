Why “Kita”? It’s Filipino for earnings, and it also means “to see”: see where your time goes, and what it’s really worth once expenses and tax come out.

Questions

Where is my data kept?

Your clients, tasks, time and invoices are private to your account and stored in a UK data centre. You can download everything as a backup from Settings at any time.

I’ve already started the year. Can I still use Kita?

Yes. Type in what each client has paid you so far, or import past invoices from a spreadsheet, and Kita includes them in your income, tax estimate and insights.

How accurate is the tax estimate?

It’s an estimate, not advice. In the UK it uses the 2026/27 rates for England, Wales and Northern Ireland, includes Class 4 National Insurance, and accounts for a salary if you add one. Like HMRC’s default cash basis, it counts income when you’re paid. It doesn’t yet cover Scottish tax bands, student loans or pension relief. In the Philippines and the US, Kita sets aside the share of profit you choose rather than calculating the tax.

Can I bill clients in another currency?

Yes. Each client and invoice can use its own currency. Kita estimates it in your currency with a daily exchange rate, and when you’re paid you record what actually arrived.

Does it work with Making Tax Digital?

Kita groups your income and expenses into the Making Tax Digital quarters and HMRC’s expense categories, and exports them as a CSV. It doesn’t send updates to HMRC directly.

Can I import meetings from my calendar?

Yes. Export your calendar as an .ics file from Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar, and Kita turns recent meetings into time entries, matching them to clients where it can. The file is read on your device and isn’t uploaded.
